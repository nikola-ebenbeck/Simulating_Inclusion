This repo combines the syntax of my simulation studies on inclusive school systems on the example of Bavaria. The regarding papers can be found here:

Study 1: Ebenbeck, N., Koch, J., Ebenbeck, J., & Gebhardt, M. (2025). What if… there were no special schools? A spatial simulation study of inclusive school systems based on governmental data of Bavaria, Germany. Zeitschrift für Grundschulforschung, 18(1), 139-156.

Study 2: Ebenbeck, N. & Gebhardt, M. (angenommen). How could special educational resources be redistributed into inclusive primary education after the closing of special schools in urban and rural areas? – A simulation study. Zeitschrift für Erziehungswissenschaft.
